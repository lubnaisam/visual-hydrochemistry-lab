2. Stiff diagrams (meq/L)

3. Interpretation and scientific cautions

Stiff polygons show the ionic composition and magnitude of selected major ions. Cations appear to the left, anions to the right. The polygon is formed from Ca–HCO₃, Mg–SO₄ and (Na+K)–Cl pairs.

Use a common scale when comparing samples. The polygon alone cannot establish potability, contamination source, or hydrochemical processes. Check ionic balance and analytical completeness first.

Method and references

Stiff, H. A. Jr. (1951). The interpretation of chemical water analysis by means of patterns. Journal of Petroleum Technology, 3(10), 15–17. DOI: 10.2118/951376-G. Plot conventions follow paired cations/anions in milliequivalents per litre. This is an educational tool; verify analytical completeness and reporting units before professional use.

Piper Diagram · Three-field hydrochemical classification

The cation and anion triangles show percentages of milliequivalents. The central diamond combines alkaline-earth/alkali and weak/strong-acid proportions. All samples share the same axes.

Ion fractions (meq %)

Cation groups: Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺, Na⁺+K⁺. Anion groups: HCO₃⁻, SO₄²⁻, Cl⁻. Each triangle is normalized separately to 100% meq. The diamond is based on (Na+K)/total cations and (SO4+Cl)/total anions. This tool does not infer potability or a unique geochemical process. Reference: Piper, A. M. (1944), A graphic procedure in the geochemical interpretation of water-analyses, Transactions, American Geophysical Union, 25, 914–928.

Schoeller Diagram · Semilogarithmic ionic profiles

Compare major-ion concentrations in meq/L on a logarithmic vertical axis. Samples share the same scale; nonpositive values cannot be plotted.

The semilogarithmic diagram shows Ca, Mg, Na+K, Cl, SO4 and HCO3 as meq/L. Zero or missing ions are omitted rather than plotted as zero on a logarithmic axis. Compare profiles only after checking analytical completeness and charge balance. Reference: Schoeller, H. (1935), Utilité de la notion des échanges de bases pour la comparaison des eaux souterraines, France.
